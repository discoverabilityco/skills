# CaseMine

**Route:** plain email to `support@casemine.com`. No form, no portal. One well-built email is the whole job.

**Eligibility:** the person named in the judgments.

**What you need before starting:**
- The exact URL of every CaseMine judgment page about the user. Judgment pages sit at `www.casemine.com/judgement/us/<long-id>`. The policy requires exact URLs; a name alone is not a request.
- With a common name: open each page and check the parties against the user's own case before it goes on the list. Sending someone else's case weakens the whole request.

**Steps (~20-30 minutes per batch email):**
1. Before writing anything, show the user the trade they are making: CaseMine implements granted de-indexes as name-search lines added to the public file at `www.casemine.com/robots.txt`. Load that file and search the user's name in quotes so they can see today's results. The choice is between the CaseMine page and a line in a public file that names them; it is their call.
2. Draft the email: every exact URL, a statement that the user is requesting de-indexing, the ground in one or two sentences, name in the body. Subject line: `<name> de-indexing request`. That subject stays identical on every follow-up; it is the only routing there is.
3. If the user holds a sealing, expungement, or protective order, attach it. Send no ID, no Social Security number, no date of birth, no money.
4. Before sending, screenshot the policy page (`www.casemine.com/home/judgment-hosting-and-takedown-policy-india`). The screenshot plus the sent email is the user's whole file if the rule ever changes.
5. The user sends it from an address they check.

**What to expect:** no ticket number, no status page, no published decision window. The privacy policy commits to responding within 30 days. After 30 silent days, resend the same email unchanged with the same subject and copy `hello@casemine.com`, the second address CaseMine publishes for data requests.

**Gotchas:**
- The written grounds are narrow: CaseMine names specific cases like matrimonial disputes and child custody. A fraud judgment, DUI appeal, discipline case, or employment suit sits outside the written gate and can be refused on policy even when well written. A court order is the only escalation it names; email a removal order as a PDF to the same address. CaseMine is India-based under Indian law; a US order does not automatically bind it.
- "Approved" is not "fixed": the de-index covers the name-search page, not the judgment page, and the judgment stays live at its URL. Verify in a browser: search the name on Google, on casemine.com, and load every URL sent.
- Nothing promises the suppression lasts. Calendar a quarterly re-check.
- Skip DMCA and privacy-law arguments; CaseMine answers both on its own policy page before they are sent. No CCPA/CPRA section exists in its policy to point at.
- GDPR route is real only for EU/UK data subjects: CaseMine's Article 27 representative is Euverify Ltd, `gdpr@euverify.com`.
