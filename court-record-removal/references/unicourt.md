**Route:** Public Records Redaction Request form at `https://unicourt.com/case/removeRecord`. Free at every stage, including the appeal; UniCourt's own policy says so and adds that nobody should be billing you for this form, us included.

**Eligibility (the real gate):** UniCourt "only considers requests submitted by a party to the public record, a legal guardian or fiduciary of a party, or an attorney representing a party." A reputation firm is not on the list: the user signs, or their attorney of record files. The form asks the relationship first; attorneys add bar number and bar state.

**What you need before starting:**
- Every UniCourt URL carrying the case: the form takes one URL per submission, so search the site for the name and the caption and list every hit. Three URLs, three requests.
- The conformed (clerk-stamped) order if the record was sealed or expunged, or a police report for physical-harm, identity-theft, or minor-harm claims. UniCourt decides on the documentation attached; do not describe the document, upload it.

**Steps (~45-60 minutes per URL):**
1. Confirm the user is one of the three eligible filers.
2. Walk the four factors honestly: sealed/expunged, physical danger, identity theft, harm to a minor. Most ordinary civil cases match none, but UniCourt's policy says the factors are not exclusive and asks for a request anyway. Send it.
3. Fill the form: record URL, relationship, name, full mailing address, email plus confirming email, phone plus confirming phone, country and state, whether sealed/expunged, stated reason, upload. Last box is a declaration under penalty of perjury, signed by the user themselves.
4. Submit. Skip the CCPA request form (`unicourt.com/pii-requests`): California-only, wants a government ID, and its own page says the CCPA does not apply to court records.

**What to expect:** decision typically within 30 days, communicated in writing. A grant de-indexes the URL, landing within about 30 more days; UniCourt does not control the search engines. Budget roughly 60 days from send to clean result, with cached snippets in between. Re-check at 60, 90, and 180 days: records have been reported coming back, and UniCourt publishes no approval rate or median turnaround.

**Gotchas:**
- Appeal within 30 days of the decision, and it must carry substantive new documentation; appeals without it are denied, and all appeals are final.
- The complaint on record with the BBB: a sealed and dismissed divorce still online, UniCourt's answer being that the docket was not sealed. Bring the stamped order, not the memory of what the judge said.
- A grant de-indexes the UniCourt URL only. It does not seal, expunge, or touch the court's own record; only a motion in the court does that.
