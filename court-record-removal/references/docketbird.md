**Route:** web form at `https://www.docketbird.com/request_case_removal?case_id=<CASE_ID>`. There is no removal link anywhere in the site menus or help; the URL is the route. Free takedown, immediate. Paid optional expedited Google submission exists inside the flow at an unpublished price.

**Eligibility:** none published; the identity check is the gate (below).

**What you need before starting:**
- The DocketBird case ID from the case page URL (federal looks like `nyed-1:2018-cv-04339`, state like `c-lasu-2025-cv-25STCV14372`).
- An email address the user controls.
- A credit or debit card whose last name matches a word in the case title. This is the entire identity check: a $1 authorization, placed and canceled, never charged. No card, or a surname mismatch (maiden name, name change, company caption, co-defendant, misspelled caption): use the mail-in route.

**Steps (online route, ~30 minutes):**
1. Open the case page, copy the ID, append it to the removal URL. The page renders "Request Removal of" plus the case title; that is how you know the ID is right.
2. Enter the user's email. DocketBird sends a confirmation code that gates every later step; keep the tab open.
3. Choose option one: free removal from DocketBird, no Google removal. Option two adds a paid expedited Google submission; the price only appears in the flow, ignore any figure read elsewhere.
4. Pass the card check.
5. Done: the takedown is immediate.

**Mail-in route (no usable card):**
- Print `https://www.docketbird.com/static/DocketBird_case_removal_request_form.pdf`. Full name, email, phone, address, case name and number, description of the user's role in the case, plus a printed copy of the DocketBird page showing the full case name and number.
- Page two is a notary acknowledgement: book the notary before filling anything in; bring photo ID.
- Mail to Mink LLC, Attention: Removals, 5940 S. Rainbow Blvd., Suite 400, PMB 66425, Las Vegas, Nevada 89118-2507. Allow 6-8 weeks. This route does not include the Google submission.

**Gotchas:**
- The free option leaves the Google result live for months. DocketBird says the case "will remain on Google indefinitely" without the Google step, and the natural de-index can take several months. After removal, still do the Google Refresh Outdated Content step.
- Paid-option users wait about five days, then check. The submission covers only the docket-sheet URL; separately ranking filings go to help@docketbird.com as a plain URL list.
- DocketBird does not verify Google actually removed anything; periodic checking is the user's job. Calendar a check one week out.
- Paid and still there after repeated effort: the Terms promise a full refund on written request to help@docketbird.com.
- A lawyer may have hidden the case through their own DocketBird account; account-based removals expire if that account is canceled, so redo it through the public removal page.
- Removal hides, not deletes: account holders (including library patrons) can still pull the record, and a notice marker may sit at the old URL.
- DocketBird's state-court data is powered by UniCourt, a separate company: clearing DocketBird does nothing to the same case on UniCourt.

**Expectation setting:** takedown is immediate, but "gone from DocketBird" is not "gone from Google." Tell the user both clocks before they start.
