# Writing the request: tone, structure, templates

Applies to every email route (CaseMine, PacerMonitor, vLex, DocketBird follow-ups) and, in compressed form, to every form's comment or reason field.

## The rules

- First person, from the requester themselves. Never impersonate, never "we", never ghostwritten-sounding third party.
- Calm and factual. No anger, no accusations, no backstory about how much the record has cost the user. Emotional context earns refusals.
- No legal threats in a first request. Where a legal basis exists, one sentence naming it is stronger than a paragraph of citations.
- Never build the request on a privacy statute. CCPA/CPRA and FCRA claims fail on court records everywhere, and CaseMine, PacerMonitor, and UniCourt publish that refusal in advance. The request is to the site's own policy.
- Exact URLs, one per line. A name alone is not a request at CaseMine or PacerMonitor.
- One sentence on who the user is: "I am the plaintiff/defendant in this case," or the relationship to the record.
- If sealed, expunged, vacated, or dismissed: one sentence saying so, plus the certified (clerk-stamped) order attached. Screenshots and docket printouts are not certifications.
- One sentence stating the ask: "Please block these pages from search engine indexing" (de-index sites) or "Please remove these pages" (removal sites). Do not ask for deletion where the site only grants de-indexing; ask for what their policy grants.
- Subject line carries the routing. CaseMine: `<name> de-indexing request`, identical on every follow-up. Others: `<name> - removal request` or the site's own convention.
- No government ID, Social Security number, date of birth, or money to any site. CaseMine and UniCourt publish that their routes are free. If a reply asks for identity documents no published step requires, redact to name and photo and send that.
- Keep a copy of everything sent, with dates. Follow-ups repeat the original unchanged except a first line: "second request, first sent <date>."

## Template: de-index request (CourtListener form message, CaseMine, general purpose)

```
Subject: <Full Name> de-indexing request

I am the person named in the pages below. Under your content removal
policy, please block them from search engine indexing.

<URL 1>
<URL 2>

I am the <plaintiff/defendant> in this case. <If applicable:> This record
was sealed/expunged by the court on <date>; the certified order is attached.

Thank you,
<Full Name>
```

For CaseMine, the subject format and identical-subject rule are mandatory; the ground sentence should be one or two sentences in the body.

## Template: removal request with documentation (PacerMonitor, vLex)

```
Subject: <Full Name> - removal request (<case number>)

To the content removal team:

I am the <plaintiff/defendant/counsel of record> in the case referenced
below. I am writing under your content removal policy to request removal
of the pages carrying my case.

<URL 1>
<URL 2>

Case: <full caption>
Court: <court>, <district>
Case number: <court format, e.g. 3:22-cv-01230>
My relationship: <party / counsel with appearance>

The case was <sealed / expunged / vacated / dismissed with prejudice> on
<date>. A certified copy of the order is attached.

<Only where a legal basis genuinely exists:> <One sentence naming the
provision relied on.>

Thank you,
<Full Name>
```

## Template: form comment box (Justia, Trellis explanation, Docket Alarm reason)

Three sentences that work, sized to the field:

1. Who: "I am the person named in these pages / I am the <plaintiff> in this case."
2. What: "I am requesting that they be blocked from search engine indexing / redacted."
3. Ground: "The record was sealed on <date> (certified order attached)" or, for Trellis-style explanation fields, the concrete checkable harm ("prospective employers running my name find this; it states <fact>").

Trellis explanations: state facts a stranger could verify, and never argue a privacy statute. Docket Alarm reasons: same shape in under 300 characters, and name the automatic categories (minor, divorce, social security) when they apply.

## Follow-up cadence

- CaseMine: 30 days, then resend unchanged + `hello@casemine.com`.
- vLex: 2 weeks, then resend unchanged with the second-request opening line.
- Everyone else: no published window; assume 1-4 weeks per site, and treat permanent silence as a no. Record it as a no and move on; an open item re-checked every Tuesday for six months is the real cost of doing this alone.
